# Pricing

Pricing should reinforce usage and audit value. Do not charge per visitor pass or per credential.

## Working Model

- Hardware kit: $499 one-time per installed gate package.
- Installation and setup: $350 one-time.
- Per gate: $79/month.
- Per unit: $0.75/month.
- Annual prepay: optional 10% SaaS discount.
- Portfolio rollout: discount only after the first property proves the value.

The hardware kit should be sold as one line item: `Angel Gates Edge Controller and Reader Kit`. Do not itemize the Pi, scanner, relay HAT, camera, SD cards, or enclosure on the customer invoice.

Installation/setup covers field wiring, mounting, commissioning, manager training, and early support. For live installs, a qualified installer should be paid from this line item rather than treated as an informal favor.

## Example: 270-Unit Property

For a one-gate, 270-unit property:

```text
$79 gate base + (270 * $0.75) = $281.50/month
```

Round the contract price to $279/month.

```text
Hardware kit:        $499 one-time
Install/setup:       $350 one-time
SaaS:                $279/month
Year-one SaaS:       $3,348
Year-one total:      $4,197
Year-two onward:     $3,348/year
```

For a first 90-day pilot, the pilot agreement can waive the hardware and setup fees while still naming the standard price. If success metrics are met, the property converts to the normal monthly SaaS price.

The pitch is not just "cheaper software." It is loss prevention, access accountability, fewer dial-pad headaches, faster visitor flow, and better chargeback evidence when gate arms are damaged.

## Loss-Prevention Math

The working sales hypothesis for the first property is that damaged gate arms, open-gate periods, and related service calls cost roughly $4,000 to $8,000 per year. That number must be validated from maintenance records before it becomes public sales copy.

The sales comparison should be stated plainly:

```text
Current pain: damaged arms, open gates, dial-pad failures, and limited chargeback evidence.
Angel Gates: $4,197 year one, $3,348/year after that for one 270-unit gate property.
Value: every gate event has timestamp, credential attribution, relay event, and video/clip evidence when cameras are present.
```

If the property can recover even part of the annual arm damage through evidence-backed chargebacks, the system can pay for itself in year one.

## Portfolio Pricing

Do not lead with discounts. Keep standard pricing as the anchor.

- 1-4 properties: standard pricing.
- 5-9 properties: 10% SaaS discount and reduced setup where installs are clustered.
- 10+ properties: 15% SaaS discount, bundled setup on qualified rollouts, and a named support contact.

Use annual prepay where possible because property managers can budget it cleanly and it improves cash flow.

## Why No Per-Credential Pricing

Per-credential or per-pass pricing makes managers avoid issuing passes. That reduces the audit trail, which is one of the main reasons the product exists.

Visitor passes should be used freely so every access decision becomes accountable.

## Competitive Position

- Gatewise and Proptia: strong in modern access workflows, but Angel Gates should lead with hardware-agnostic retrofit and edge-first reliability.
- ButterflyMX: strong multifamily/intercom product, but Angel Gates should avoid making video intercom the v1 problem.
- Brivo and PDK: broader access-control platforms, but Angel Gates should be narrower and sharper for gate retrofits.
- OEM clouds: useful later, but the v1 compatibility story is dry contact/Wiegand-style retrofit, not waiting on cloud APIs.

## Pricing Principle

The property is the tenant. Gates are endpoints. Credentials are usage, not a toll booth.
