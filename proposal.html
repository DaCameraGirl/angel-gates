The simple pitch

A cleaner gate experience with better accountability.

The current DoorKing setup remains in place. Angel Gates is a value-add asset beside it, giving residents and managers a modern way to send time-limited QR/PIN passes to guests, deliveries, cleaners, vendors, contractors, and family.

The point is faster gate flow on normal days and better evidence on bad days. When something goes wrong, the property gets a timestamped event that can be matched to camera footage and delivered to the property manager for review.

Current pain

The old metal box stays, but it should not be the only visitor workflow.

Loud failed-code lines

Visitor pass flow

Six steps from pass to proof

1

Create

Resident or manager creates a pass for a guest, vendor, delivery, or contractor.

2

Limit

The pass gets a gate, sponsor, start time, expiration time, and QR/PIN credential.

3

Send

The visitor receives the pass before arriving at the gate.

4

Scan

The visitor scans QR or enters PIN at the Angel Gates reader.

5

Open

Angel Gates validates locally, logs the event, and sends the open command.

6

Review

If there is an incident, the manager can match pass, sponsor, time, and camera footage.

Incident email

Every incident becomes a timestamped manager email.

Angel Gates can use the property's existing camera or NVR if it provides an approved stream, snapshot, or clip export. If the existing camera already sees the gate arm and vehicle area, Angel Gates can use that as the evidence source. The system packages the time, gate event, credential, sponsor, vehicle/tag reference, and camera clip into a manager-ready review email for damage, possible unauthorized entry, denied access, or other incidents.

Install shape

A jewel asset beside DoorKing, not a replacement.

DoorKing stays

A premium access-and-evidence asset: edge controller, QR/PIN reader, local event log, camera evidence links, and manager email workflow.
